I am a PhD student in Mathematics at <a href="https://matematicas.ucm.es" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Universidad Complutense de Madrid</a>, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://www.ucm.es/imi/rutwig-campoamor-stursberg" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Rutwig Campoamor-Stursberg</a> (UCM) and Prof. <a href="https://investigacion.ubu.es/investigadores/35246/detalle" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Francisco José Herranz</a> (UBU).

I am affiliated with the <a href="https://www.ucm.es/algebra_geometria_topologia" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Department of Algebra, Geometry and Topology</a> and the <a href="https://www.ucm.es/imi/" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Interdisciplinary Mathematics Institute</a> at UCM, and I am a member of the <a href="https://gmcnet.webs.ull.es" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Geometry, Dynamics and Field Theory Network</a> and the <a href="https://rsme.es" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Royal Spanish Mathematical Society</a>.

My research interests lie in the areas of Lie theory, differential geometry, and differential equations. Specifically, my ongoing PhD project focuses on Lie systems on curved spaces with compatible geometric structures.
