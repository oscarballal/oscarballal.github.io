I am a PhD student in Mathematics at <a href="https://matematicas.ucm.es" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Universidad Complutense de Madrid</a>, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://www.ucm.es/imi/rutwig-campoamor-stursberg" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Rutwig Campoamor-Stursberg</a> (UCM) and Prof. <a href="https://investigacion.ubu.es/investigadores/35246/detalle" style="color: #8C6D17; text-decoration: underline; border-bottom: none;">Francisco José Herranz</a> (UBU).

My research interests lie in the areas of Lie theory, differential geometry, and differential equations. Specifically, my ongoing PhD project is about Lie systems on curved spaces with compatible geometric structures.
