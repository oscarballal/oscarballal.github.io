**Authors:** Oscar Carballal, Rutwig Campoamor-Stursberg, Francisco José Herranz

### Abstract

We investigate the integrability of Lie systems defined on curved manifolds endowed with compatible geometric structures. Our main result establishes that the associated superposition rule satisfies the following geometric condition:

$$ \mathcal{L}_X \omega = 0 $$
